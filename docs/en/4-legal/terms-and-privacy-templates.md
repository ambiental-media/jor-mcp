# **JOR-MCP Terms of Use and Privacy Policy Templates**

These templates are a starting point for organizations that operate their own JOR-MCP instance. They were prepared in the Brazilian legal context and are made available as reference material, as is, with no guarantee of suitability for any specific organization or situation.

This material does not constitute legal advice. Before publishing, review the documents with a lawyer you trust and adapt them to the way your organization actually operates.

The English versions are reference translations. In case of any discrepancy, the Portuguese version prevails.

Each organization is solely responsible for the documents it publishes and for the instance it operates. Ambiental Media does not operate third-party instances, is not responsible for them, and is not a party to the relationship between your organization and the users of your service.

## **How to use**

1. Fill in the fields in square brackets.  
2. Decide, with your lawyer, whether to keep or delete the clauses marked \[OPTIONAL\].  
3. Keep the clause on the development of JOR-MCP (Terms, clause 1.2, and the preamble of the Policy). It tells your users who is responsible for the service.
