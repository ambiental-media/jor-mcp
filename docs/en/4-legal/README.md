## Before publishing these templates

- **Fill in every field in square brackets.** They identify the organization, the service name, the contacts, and the dates.
- **Decide on the optional clauses.** Clauses marked `[OPTIONAL]` may be kept or deleted depending on how your organization actually operates.
- **Keep the clause about Jor-MCP.** Terms, clause 1.2, and the preamble of the Policy state who is responsible for the service.
- **Review with a lawyer.** These documents are reference material in the Brazilian legal context, provided as is, and do not constitute legal advice.

## Scope and responsibility

- Ambiental Media develops and maintains Jor-MCP as open-source software, but does **not** operate third-party instances and is **not** a party to the relationship between an operator and its users.
- Each organization is solely responsible for the documents it publishes and for the instance it operates.
- The English versions are reference translations. In case of any discrepancy, the Portuguese version prevails.
